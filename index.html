Questions, answered

What's a go-around?

An airliner on final approach climbs away instead of landing and flies another approach — a routine safety procedure when a runway isn't clear or an approach isn't stable. Spotters call it a go-around or a missed approach. They're unplanned, they're dramatic, and they're uncommon enough that catching one live is special.

Which airports will be covered?

We're launching with Los Angeles (LAX), New York (JFK), London Heathrow (LHR), Frankfurt (FRA) and Amsterdam (AMS). Each airport gets its own free public channel, and more airports follow as the service grows.

How do alerts arrive?

Free: public Telegram channels and Discord feeds, with a short delay. Instant: private alerts delivered the moment it happens. Every alert — free or Instant — shows the airline, aircraft type, airport and time, with direct links to the aircraft on ADS-B Exchange and FlightRadar24.

What's the difference between Free and Instant?

Free channels post the go-arounds we detect at each covered airport with a short delay. Instant is $4.99/month or $39.99/year: alerts the moment it happens, up to 10 airports at once, and airline and aircraft-type filters.

Do I need an app?

No. Alerts arrive in Telegram or Discord — join the free channels or connect your account for Instant alerts and you're set.

Are you affiliated with ADS-B Exchange or FlightRadar24?

No — we're an independent service. Alerts link to the aircraft's pages on ADS-B Exchange and FlightRadar24 so you can pull them up and watch; we're not affiliated with or endorsed by either.
